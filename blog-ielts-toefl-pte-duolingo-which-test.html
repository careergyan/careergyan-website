IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo: Which English Test Should You Take?

Career Gyan Team18 Sep 20265 min read

Not sure which English test to book? Compare IELTS, TOEFL, PTE and Duolingo on format, results time and where each one is accepted.

Almost every university abroad asks for proof of English. Choosing the right test saves you money and stress. The best test is the one your universities and your visa office accept, in a format that suits you.

IELTS Academic

The most widely accepted test for universities and visas, especially in the UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Canada and Europe. You can take it on paper or on a computer, and the speaking test is a face-to-face conversation with an examiner. Results usually arrive within a few days for the computer test.

TOEFL iBT

Popular with US universities and widely accepted in Europe and Canada. It is fully computer based, and the speaking section is recorded. Good for students who are comfortable with academic reading and listening on screen.

PTE Academic

A computer-based test scored by AI, known for fast results, often within two days. It is accepted by most universities in Australia, New Zealand and the UK, and by many in Europe, Canada and the USA.

Duolingo English Test

An online test you can take from home in about an hour, at a lower fee. Many universities in the USA, Canada and Europe accept it for admission, but it is not accepted for every visa or every university. Always check before booking.

How to decide

  • Shortlist your universities first, then check which tests they accept and the minimum scores.
  • Check the visa rules too. The UK, for example, requires approved tests for some visa routes.
  • Pick the format that suits you: paper or computer, human examiner or recorded speaking.
  • Allow time for a retake before your application deadlines.
