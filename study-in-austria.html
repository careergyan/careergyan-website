Why Study in Austria?

Why study in Austria

Austria offers students the opportunity to experience high-quality European education at comparatively affordable tuition fees, along with a safe, multicultural and internationally connected environment. The country has a strong tradition of higher education, internationally oriented institutions and a growing range of English-taught programmes.

Austria's higher-education system has 77 institutions: universities, universities of applied sciences (Fachhochschulen) and teacher training colleges. Public universities charge non-EU students €726.72* per semester, one of the lowest tuition fees in Western Europe, and around 580 programmes are taught in English.

So, along with a world-class degree, you get to live in the heart of Europe, in one of the safest countries in the world, with easy travel to Germany, Italy, Switzerland and beyond.

Eligibility

Who can apply to study in Austria

Eligibility depends on the programme and the university, and each university checks your documents individually. The general requirements to study in Austria are:

01

Bachelor's

A school-leaving certificate recognised as equivalent to the Austrian Matura (general university entrance qualification). Some universities accept the Indian Class 12 certificate, while others ask for an entrance examination or completed university study. Your counsellor checks the rules of your chosen university.

02

Master's

A completed bachelor's degree (usually 3 years / 180 ECTS or more) in a related subject. Some universities, such as the University of Graz, ask Indian applicants for a document competence check through VFS Global.

03

English

English-taught programmes usually ask for B2 level or higher, for example IELTS Academic 6.0–7.5 or TOEFL iBT 80+, depending on the programme.

04

German

German-taught programmes need German at B2–C1 level. Many universities offer a preparatory German course (Vorstudienlehrgang) before you start.

05

Entrance procedures

Universities of applied sciences and popular programmes such as medicine, psychology and business have limited places, so they hold an entrance test or interview.

Visa & documents

What you'll need to apply

Visa requirements

  • Residence Permit – Student, applied for at the Austrian Embassy or Consulate at least 3 months before travel (6 months advised)
  • Admission letter (Zulassungsbescheid) from your Austrian university
  • Proof of funds for 12 months: €722.58* per month (under 24) or €1,308.39* per month (24 and over)
  • Accommodation in Austria for at least 3 months
  • Health insurance covering all risks (ÖGK student self-insurance: €78.84* per month)
  • Police clearance certificate, legalised and translated into German
  • Application fee: €218* (one-time)

Application documents

  • Valid passport (copy of all pages)
  • Passport photo (3.5 x 4.5 cm)
  • Admission letter (Zulassungsbescheid)
  • Academic transcripts and certificates (legalised)
  • English or German language certificate
  • Statement of Purpose / Motivation letter
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Proof of financial means
  • Health insurance
  • Proof of accommodation
  • Police clearance certificate
